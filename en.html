Lexi Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 26, 2026 · Last updated: June 26, 2026

Lexi (the "Service") values your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information the Service collects, how it is used, and how it is protected and deleted.

1. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

The Service collects the following personal information:

CategoryData collectedWhen / how
Social login information Email address, name, and profile information (provided by your Google or Apple account) At sign-up / login (Google / Apple OAuth)
Profile information Nickname, identifier tag (#NNNNNN), profile image (avatar) Generated automatically at sign-up, or entered/uploaded by the user
Learning & usage data Experience points (XP) and level, in-app currencies and unlocked items (spaceships, backgrounds, planets), entitlement tier, last login time Generated and stored automatically during use of the Service
User-generated content Words, meanings, example sentences, and decks shared within groups ("Squares") When a user shares content to a group

Note: Decks you create are stored locally on your device by default and may be backed up for same-account device restore depending on your operating system settings (iOS iCloud / Android Auto Backup). Decks that are not shared to a group are not transmitted to our servers.

Note: If you sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email," we may receive an anonymized Apple Private Relay address instead of your real email address.

2. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • Member identification, login/authentication, and account management
  • Storing learning progress (XP, level, unlocked items, etc.) and syncing it across devices
  • Providing content-sharing features between users, such as groups (Squares)
  • Verifying entitlement (premium) tier and providing related benefits
  • Operating and improving the Service, preventing abuse, and responding to inquiries

3. Retention and Use Period

  • As a rule, we retain and use personal information until you withdraw your membership.
  • When you request account deletion, related data is permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period. You can cancel the deletion by logging back in during this period.
  • Where retention is required by applicable law, we retain the data for the period required by that law and then destroy it.

4. Third-Party Processing

We do not sell your personal information. We process information through the following trusted providers only to the extent necessary to operate the Service:

ProviderPurpose
Supabase Inc.Storage and management of member data (backend infrastructure) and authentication
Google LLCSocial login (OAuth) authentication
Apple Inc.Social login (Sign in with Apple) authentication

Note: If paid features are introduced in the future, additional providers for payment processing (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat) may be added, and we will notify you in advance through this policy.

5. Deletion Procedure and Method

  • You can request deletion of your account and personal information directly via [Settings → Delete Account] in the app.
  • Upon request, your account is deactivated immediately, and after a 30-day grace period the related data is permanently and irrecoverably deleted from our servers.
  • Information stored as electronic files is deleted using technical methods that prevent recovery or reproduction.

6. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • You may view and edit your personal information at any time and may request account deletion (suspension of processing).
  • Items such as your nickname and profile image can be changed directly in the app settings.
  • Other privacy-related requests can be handled through the contact below.

7. Security Measures

  • All communications are transmitted encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database access is minimized, and per-user data access is controlled using mechanisms such as Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Authentication and login are handled through trusted external providers (OAuth); the Service does not store the passwords of your social accounts.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is amended by addition, deletion, or modification, we will announce the changes on this page. For significant changes, we will provide notice before the effective date.
